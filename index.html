The Problem in Conventional Grinding

Commercial disc and burr grinders rely on single-pass open-loop comminution, generating a broad bimodal particle distribution:

  • Boulders (> 1000 µm): Low surface area per unit volume; water channels past them, resulting in under-extraction and sour, weak brew.
  • Fines (< 100 µm): Frictional dust particles migrate during percolation, blinding filter pores and creating erratic pressure surges (Ergun equation resistance). They over-extract rapidly, leaching astringent tannins.
  • Aroma Loss: Grinding temperatures exceed 55°C, stripping volatile aromatics.
  • Material Loss: 15%–22% of product is discarded or rejected as out-of-spec.
✨

The BrewDz Closed-Loop Innovation

BrewDz implements a closed-circuit fluid-particle system engineered for high-precision coffee powder manufacture:

  • Low-Shear Chilled Roller Milling: Dual-roll differential shear with water-cooled jackets keeps temperature < 28°C.
  • Multi-Deck Vibratory Classification: High-frequency vibratory screen cuts oversize boulders and separates dust.
  • Automated Oversize Recycle: 100% of out-of-spec oversize is routed back into the mill feed, pushing total product yield to 96%+.
  • Aerodynamic Fines Recovery: Super-fines are harvested via cyclone air classification as high-solids instant coffee feed.
